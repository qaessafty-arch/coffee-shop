Our Story

Founded in the heart of the city of Erbil, ODOR was born from a simple passion: to bring the world's finest beans to your cup. We believe in fair trade, small-batch roasting, and the art of slowing down to enjoy the moment.

"Here in Erbil, where the ancient Citadel watches over our streets, we hold tight to one simple belief: 'Coffee is the language of connection.' We invite you to pause, take a seat, and let the rich aromas wash over you. After all, every cup we pour is a quiet conversation between old traditions and new dreams."
